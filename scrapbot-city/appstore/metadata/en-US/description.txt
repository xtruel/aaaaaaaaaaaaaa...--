Small robots, big choices.

The Scrap Coast is buried in junk. Your little scrap bots fly out, collect it and bring it home, and what it becomes is up to you. Recycle it into parts, melt it into credits, power the city with solar panels or scrap burners, and bring in citizens who pay taxes… and make more trash.

BUILD A CITY THAT WORKS
• 12 buildings, from the Recycler and the Bot Dock to the 2PM Factory and the Scrap Spire monument.
• Keep energy above demand, or everything slows down.
• Industry pays fast but makes smog: clean it with Pixel Gardens and Purifiers before it reaches 95%. Stay above it for 30 seconds and the city is lost.
• Every building's panel shows its real numbers, at full power and right now, and says in words what is slowing it down.
• See where the smog comes from: clouds over the polluting buildings, a haze over the city as the air gets worse, and a breakdown of every source and every cleaner, per day.

GREEN OR INDUSTRY?
Every building you keep and every choice you make in story events moves the path meter, between green and industrial. Finish the Scrap Spire and the ending depends on where you stand: The Garden City, The 2PM Megacity, or Different Futures, Same City.

THE SCRAP COAST CAMPAIGN
• Five cities on a region map, each with its own rules: a harbour of shipwrecks, neon towers over toxic sludge, the last green valley, a company town owned by 2PM Corp.
• Three stars to earn in every city; stars unlock the next ones.
• A cargo blimp moves credits and parts between your cities (it keeps 10%).
• Story events with USA-9, Miso the cat, K. Kurogane of 2PM Corp and the citizens.
• Free play: a city with the name and seed you choose. A guided tutorial with USA-9.

SHAPE YOUR NEIGHBOURHOODS: OBJECTS & MODULES
• 24 items in the catalog: 6 constructions (industrial floor, fence, gate, concrete base, low wall, corner pillar), 10 decorations (lamp post, bench, planter, neon sign, pergola, Miso's house, scrap sculpture, tree, fountain, statue of USA-9) and 8 machine modules.
• 6 are ready from the start. The other 18 are projects to discover by reaching milestones in any city, in the campaign or in free play.
• Constructions and decorations are for looks only: no effect on the economy, smog or stars. Lamp posts and neon signs light up at night, and decorations can be moved after they are placed.
• A module changes how one building works, with a trade-off you can read before you buy: one module per building.

A CITY THAT LIVES
• Isometric pixel art: bots flying over the roofs, citizens on the sidewalks, Miso the cat, the 2PM blimp.
• Day and night: windows, screens, furnaces and street lamps light up after dark.
• Rustbay, Neon Heights, Greenvale and 2PM Company Town each have their own ground, scenery and light.

OFFLINE, NO ADS
• Plays offline. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected.
• 8 languages: English, Italiano, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português, Polski, Nederlands.
• Automatic saves every in-game day and when you leave the app; three manual slots in free play.
